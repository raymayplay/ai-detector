Who made this, and why trust it?

AI Video Detector was created by Moksh Oswal to make synthetic-media checks easier to inspect. It is a transparent screening tool, not a court-grade authenticity certificate.

Visible evidence Every result shows the score, confidence, signals, and—when available—frame measurements.
No fake accuracy claim Accuracy is shown only from an independently labeled benchmark, with the sample count and confusion matrix.
Known limits Compression, missing metadata, editing, and new generators can fool heuristic detectors. Important claims need human review.
